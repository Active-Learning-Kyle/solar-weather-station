## Avoiding sunk-cost bias

The existence of a student-built prototype is not a reason to continue building it. The correct direction depends on whether the primary objective is dependable weather data or engineering learning.

## Objective-dependent decision

### When reliable local weather data is the objective

Buy or borrow mature commercial equipment, especially for research, long-term monitoring, decision support, traceability and low-maintenance use. Rebuilding a solved measurement service only because development has started is not a defensible engineering decision.

### When engineering education is the objective

Continue a student-built prototype because sensing, integration, power, communication, enclosure, field failure, evidence and redesign are the learning outcome. Its role must remain `student-built educational / experimental prototype`, not an assumed meteorological instrument.

## Provisional preferred strategy

Use a reliable commercial or borrowed instrument as the reference and, where reliable weather data is required, as the primary data source. Continue development of the student-built weather station as a modular educational prototype, subject to successful safety, field-testing and validation gates.

| System | Primary role |
|---|---|
| Commercial/borrowed instrument | trusted comparison baseline; primary source where dependable data is required; reference metadata and accuracy context |
| Student-built modular prototype | hands-on integration, IoT, solar/low-power design, logging, enclosure, troubleshooting, modification and evidence-led learning |

The higher-value student question becomes: **Can we design a low-cost, open and modular system and use reference evidence to show the conditions under which it is sufficiently reliable?**

## Green Technology / ESG boundary

A solar panel does not by itself earn a sustainability advantage. Future Green Technology scoring should use evidence for:

- measured energy consumption and solar contribution;
- battery performance;
- material/component choice;
- repairability and modularity;
- expected lifetime and reuse; and
- the actual problem solved compared with a commercial baseline.

Until then, describe the project as a platform for learning and exploring low-power environmental sensing and sustainable engineering design—not as a proven Green Technology or ESG solution.

## Evidence required before a real procurement/deployment decision

- independent TA completion/alignment for Gate D;
- independent matrix scores and recorded disagreements;
- sensitivity test for the two most disputed weights;
- current HKU/Hong Kong loan availability and quotations;
- selected commercial/reference instrument and comparison method;
- real site evidence if physical deployment is pursued; and
- a dated revisit trigger if prototype accuracy, safety, maintenance or educational value changes.
