# v0.2 Preview Enclosure and Mounting Specification

> Design specification only. No CAD, print, ingress, UV, thermal, wind-load or field evidence is claimed.

## Three-zone concept

| Zone | Contents | Required separation/control |
|---|---|---|
| Metrology zone | SHT45/BMP390 radiation shield and LTR390 optical head | shaded ventilated air path; no controller/power heat; defined UVA window |
| Control zone | ESP32-S3, RS-485 interface and microSD | serviceable dry volume; labelled connectors; breathable pressure/condensation control |
| Power zone | manager, fuse/switching and restrained battery | protected from public access; separate inspection; no water path over battery/electronics |

## Release geometry inputs

- maximum overall footprint and height recorded before site approval;
- solar-panel area and lever arm included in wind/fall review;
- weighted freestanding base preferred for the first temporary deployment;
- complete fall zone must stay within a controlled area;
- cables never cross a walking route and enter through downward-facing glands with drip loops;
- sensor head stays clear of wall/exhaust/reflective heat and rain-runoff bias.

## Fabrication baseline

- ASA preferred for outdoor UV/weather exposure; PETG acceptable for supervised short preview tests when limitations are recorded;
- stainless or corrosion-resistant removable fasteners;
- replaceable gasket and membrane vent rather than permanent adhesive closure;
- captive or retained service fasteners where practical;
- printed part ID, revision, units, material, orientation and settings stored beside future CAD.

## Inspection and test sequence

1. dimensional/fit inspection;
2. 20 service open-close cycles;
3. cable pull/strain-relief check;
4. unpowered controlled spray test with absorbent ingress indicators;
5. sun/heat bias comparison using a co-located reference;
6. condensation observation across warm-humid/cool transition;
7. mounting ballast, tip and controlled wind-area review;
8. post-test inspection for water, deformation, corrosion, loosening and contamination.

No “IP-rated,” “weatherproof,” “robust” or typhoon-resilient claim may be made from this specification.
