## Email draft

Dear Innovation Wing Office / Technical Team,

I am preparing a Solar Weather Station as a live engineering exemplar for ENGG2202 Green Technology. Before any outdoor test, I would like to identify the correct site owner/approval route and conduct a short site walk with the responsible staff member.

The proposed first test would be:

- a supervised, temporary 72-hour prototype field validation;
- adjacent to Innovation Wing One only if a suitable and approved point exists;
- installed using a reversible freestanding tripod/weighted base where permitted;
- small-footprint, with no drilling or permanent modification;
- locally logging data so continuous Wi-Fi is not assumed; and
- inspected daily, with explicit weather/safety stop and safe-removal arrangements.

At this stage we are not requesting approval for a pre-selected point. We would appreciate your help to:

1. confirm whether Innovation Wing manages any suitable outdoor or semi-open candidate point;
2. identify the responsible site approver or route us to Faculty/building/Estates/safety staff where necessary;
3. arrange a 30–45 minute site walk to review Candidate A (more open outdoor exposure) and Candidate B (more controlled semi-open exposure);
4. clarify permitted mounting, footprint/height, normal and after-hours access, public/security restrictions, weather-triggered removal and allowable site documentation; and
5. advise whether a commercial weather station or handheld reference instrument may be borrowed or compared nearby.

We can bring an approximate configuration envelope (dimensions, mass, tripod/base and solar-panel area) and a short site-walk checklist. We will not deploy until the responsible site role and written permission/conditions are confirmed.

Could you please advise the appropriate contact and a suitable time for the site walk?
